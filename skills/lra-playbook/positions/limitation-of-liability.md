## Position
Supplier's liability capped at the greater of 200% of the charges paid or
payable in the 12 months before the claim and a fixed floor. Indemnities,
breach of confidentiality, data protection breaches, wilful default and
anything that cannot be limited by law sit outside the cap. Customer's
liability limited to the charges due.

## Fallback
Cap at 100% of 12 months' charges, with the indemnities, confidentiality and
data protection breaches still outside it. A separate, higher cap for data
protection breaches is acceptable in place of an uncapped position.

## Walk-away
A cap below 100% of 12 months' charges. Indemnities or confidentiality inside
the cap. Exclusion of "loss of data" where the supplier hosts the data.

## Watch for
- The exclusion list: "loss of profit, revenue, business, goodwill, data and
  indirect loss" quietly turns every real loss into an excluded one. Loss of
  data must not be excluded where the supplier holds it.
- A cap measured on "charges paid" alone, which is zero in month one.
- An aggregate cap for the whole term rather than per year.
- "To the extent permitted by law" doing no work.

## Model wording (fallback)
Subject to the exclusions in this clause, each party's total aggregate
liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement in any Contract
Year shall not exceed an amount equal to the Charges paid or payable in that
Contract Year. Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either party's
liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, breach of
the confidentiality obligations, breach of the data protection obligations, or
under the indemnities.
