## Position
Payment 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice. Charges fixed for the minimum
term, then increases capped at the lower of CPI and 3% a year on 90 days'
notice. Disputed amounts withheld in good faith without interest or suspension.

## Fallback
30 days from invoice. Increases capped at CPI plus 2%. Interest at 2% above
base on undisputed late amounts after a written reminder.

## Walk-away
Payment in advance for services not yet delivered beyond one quarter. Uncapped
annual increases or increases "at the Supplier's discretion". Suspension of
services for a disputed invoice.

## Watch for
- Interest at statutory rates plus a percentage, running from the invoice date
  rather than the due date.
- "Fees are non-refundable" alongside a supplier right to terminate for
  convenience.
- Charges in one currency and a cap in another.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt. The
Supplier may increase the Charges once in any 12-month period, on not less than
90 days' written notice, by no more than the percentage increase in the
Consumer Prices Index over the preceding 12 months plus 2%. The Customer may
withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, and the Supplier
shall not suspend the Services on account of a disputed amount.
