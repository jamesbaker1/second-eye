## Position
Mutual obligations, surviving for five years after termination and
indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data. Standard exclusions (public
domain, already known, independently developed, required by law with notice).
Return or destruction on request, with a certificate.

## Fallback
Survival of three years for ordinary confidential information. Disclosure to
professional advisers and affiliates under equivalent obligations.

## Walk-away
Obligations running only from the customer. Survival shorter than two years.
An exclusion for information "not marked confidential" where the customer
discloses orally or in systems that cannot be marked.

## Watch for
- "Confidential Information" defined by marking alone.
- A residuals clause that lets the supplier use whatever its staff remember.
- Confidentiality obligations that end on termination rather than surviving it.

## Model wording (fallback)
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential,
shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it
except to those of its employees, affiliates and professional advisers who
need to know it for those purposes and are bound by obligations of
confidentiality no less protective than these. These obligations survive
termination of this Agreement for three years, and indefinitely in respect of
trade secrets and personal data.
