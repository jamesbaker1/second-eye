## Position
Supplier maintains professional indemnity, public liability, employer's
liability and cyber cover at levels at least equal to the liability cap, with
reputable insurers, for the term and six years after, and provides certificates
on request.

## Fallback
Cover at the greater of a stated minimum and 50% of the cap, with run-off for
three years.

## Walk-away
No insurance obligation where the supplier handles customer data or works on
customer premises. Cover "as the Supplier considers appropriate".

## Watch for
- Insurance obligations without a right to see the certificate.
- Cyber cover missing where the supplier hosts or processes data.
- Limits stated "in the aggregate" for the whole policy year across all
  clients.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Supplier shall maintain in force, with reputable insurers, for the term of
this Agreement and for three years afterwards, professional indemnity, public
liability, employer's liability and cyber liability insurance each with a
limit of not less than the amount stated in the Schedule, and shall provide
certificates of insurance on request.
