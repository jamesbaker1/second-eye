## Position
Supplier indemnifies for third-party IP infringement claims, breach of
confidentiality and data protection obligations, and death, personal injury or
property damage caused by its negligence. Customer gives no indemnity beyond
paying the charges.

## Fallback
A mutual IP indemnity where the customer supplies materials the supplier must
use. The IP indemnity may carry the usual exclusions (customer modifications,
combinations, use outside the documentation) and a remedy of modify, replace or
refund.

## Walk-away
A customer indemnity for the supplier's own negligence or for "any claim
arising from use of the services". An indemnity capped below the supplier's
insurance. An indemnity with no duty to defend and no control of the defence
for the indemnified party.

## Watch for
- Indemnities that sit inside the liability cap: the cap swallows them.
- "Indemnify and hold harmless" running only one way in a clause headed
  "Mutual indemnities".
- Conditions (notice within N days, sole control, no admissions) so strict
  that the indemnity is lost on a technicality.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Supplier shall defend the Customer against any claim that the Services or
Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights, and shall
pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement, provided that the
Customer notifies the Supplier promptly of the claim, does not admit liability
without the Supplier's consent, and gives the Supplier reasonable assistance at
the Supplier's cost. This indemnity does not apply to the extent a claim arises
from materials supplied by the Customer or from use of the Services other than
in accordance with the Documentation.
