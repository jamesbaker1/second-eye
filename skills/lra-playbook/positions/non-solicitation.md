## Position
No non-solicitation obligation on the customer. If the supplier insists, a
mutual clause limited to staff who worked on the engagement, for six months
after they last did, excluding responses to general advertising.

## Fallback
Twelve months, still limited to engaged staff and still excluding general
advertising. A liquidated sum capped at three months' salary in place of an
injunction.

## Walk-away
A clause covering all of the supplier's staff. Any period over twelve months.
Liquidated damages of a year's salary or more.

## Watch for
- "Solicit or employ": the "employ" half catches staff who apply unprompted.
- Obligations that survive for the full period after termination of a
  multi-year agreement, so they run for years.

## Model wording (fallback)
During the term of this Agreement and for 12 months afterwards, neither party
shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was
materially engaged in the Services, provided that this does not prevent either
party from employing a person who responds to a general advertisement or
approaches it without solicitation.
