---
clause: Assignment and change of control
status: starter
side: customer
updated: 2026-09-26
---

## Position
Customer may assign or novate to an affiliate or a successor to the business
without consent. Supplier may not assign, subcontract or novate without the
customer's prior written consent. A change of control of the supplier gives
the customer a right to terminate.

## Fallback
Supplier may subcontract to named or approved subcontractors, remaining liable
for their acts. Assignment by either party to an affiliate on notice.

## Walk-away
Supplier free to assign to anyone. Customer needing consent to assign to a
successor. A change-of-control right that runs only in the supplier's favour.

## Watch for
- Subcontracting permitted "in the ordinary course" without any liability for
  the subcontractor.
- Change of control defined so narrowly (share sale only) that an asset sale
  escapes it.
- Consent "not to be unreasonably withheld" where the customer needs a genuine
  right to say no.

## Model wording (fallback)
Neither party may assign, novate or otherwise transfer any of its rights or
obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other
party, except that either party may assign or novate this Agreement to an
Affiliate on written notice. The Supplier may subcontract the performance of
the Services to the subcontractors listed in the Schedule, or to others with
the Customer's prior written consent, and shall remain liable for the acts and
omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.
