## Position
Governing law and exclusive jurisdiction of the customer's home jurisdiction.
An escalation step (senior executives, 20 business days) before proceedings,
without prejudice to injunctive relief.

## Fallback
The law of England and Wales or the State of New York with a neutral seat, if
the customer is elsewhere. Arbitration under ICC or LCIA rules, seated in
London or New York, where the supplier is outside the customer's jurisdiction.

## Walk-away
The supplier's home law and courts where the supplier is in a jurisdiction the
firm does not practise in. Mandatory arbitration with no carve-out for
injunctive relief or for undisputed debts.

## Watch for
- Governing law and jurisdiction pointing at different places.
- A jurisdiction clause that is exclusive for the customer and non-exclusive
  for the supplier.
- Waivers of jury trial or class action buried in the boilerplate.

## Model wording (fallback)
This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with
it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and
Wales. Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales
shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim, save
that either party may seek injunctive or other interim relief in any court of
competent jurisdiction.
