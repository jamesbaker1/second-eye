## Position
Customer may terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice with no termination
fee after any minimum term. Either party may terminate for material breach not
remedied within 30 days, and immediately for insolvency. On termination the
supplier assists with transition at the rates in the agreement for up to six
months and returns or deletes data on request.

## Fallback
Termination for convenience on 90 days' notice, or a termination fee limited to
charges for the unexpired minimum term at a discount. A 30-day cure period that
the supplier can extend once by written agreement.

## Walk-away
No right for the customer to terminate for convenience at any point. Supplier
termination for convenience on less than 90 days' notice. Termination fees
equal to the full charges for the whole remaining term. No transition
assistance.

## Watch for
- "Material breach" defined to require an unremedied breach of a "material
  term" that is nowhere listed.
- Automatic renewal with a notice window that has already passed by the time
  anyone looks.
- Survival clauses that keep the customer's payment obligations alive and let
  the supplier's obligations lapse.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time after the
Minimum Term by giving not less than 90 days' written notice to the Supplier.
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written
notice if the other party commits a material breach which, if capable of
remedy, is not remedied within 30 days of a written notice requiring it to be
remedied.
