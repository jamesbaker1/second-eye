## Position
Customer owns deliverables created for it and all of its data and inputs. The
supplier keeps its pre-existing materials and tools and grants a perpetual,
royalty-free licence to use them as part of the deliverables. No licence from
the customer to the supplier beyond what is needed to perform.

## Fallback
Supplier owns generic deliverables and grants the customer a perpetual,
irrevocable, worldwide licence to use, modify and sublicense them for its
business. Customer owns bespoke deliverables and its data.

## Walk-away
Supplier ownership of anything derived from customer data. A licence to the
customer that ends on termination. Supplier rights to use customer data for
"improving the services" without anonymisation and consent.

## Watch for
- "Feedback" clauses that hand over every suggestion the customer makes.
- Aggregated or "derived" data carved out of customer data.
- Licences that are perpetual but not irrevocable, or worldwide but not
  sublicensable to affiliates and outsourcers.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Customer owns all Customer Data and all Deliverables created specifically
for the Customer. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing materials
and general-purpose tools and grants the Customer a perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy, modify and sublicense them to the
extent they are incorporated in the Deliverables or necessary to use them.
