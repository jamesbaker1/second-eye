## Position
Supplier acts as processor on the customer's documented instructions, under
Article 28 terms in a schedule: sub-processors only with prior notice and a
right to object, international transfers only with an approved mechanism,
assistance with data subject requests and breach notification within 24 hours,
audit rights, and deletion or return on termination. Liability for data
protection breaches outside or above the general cap.

## Fallback
Breach notification within 48 hours. Sub-processor changes on 30 days' notice
with a right to terminate rather than a veto. Audit by questionnaire and
third-party reports, with an on-site audit after a breach or on a regulator's
request.

## Walk-away
No processing terms at all where personal data is processed. Notification "as
soon as practicable" with no outer limit. Data protection liability inside a
low general cap.

## Watch for
- "Controller" and "processor" mislabelled so that the supplier can decide
  purposes.
- Transfer clauses that name a mechanism that no longer exists.
- Deletion "in accordance with the Supplier's retention policy" instead of on
  request.

## Model wording (fallback)
The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event
within 48 hours, on becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach, shall give the
Customer not less than 30 days' written notice of any proposed change of
Sub-processor, and shall on the Customer's request return or delete all
Customer Personal Data on termination of the Services.
