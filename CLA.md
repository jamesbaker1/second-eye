# Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0

> **This is a template.** It is modelled on the Apache Software Foundation's
> Individual and Corporate CLAs and on the Harmony agreements, adapted for a
> project that is dual-licensed. It will be reviewed by a lawyer before the
> first outside contribution is accepted, and may change in that review;
> until then, no outside contribution is merged.

Thank you for contributing to Second Eye. This agreement sets out the
rights you give the project in what you contribute. It does **not** transfer
your copyright: you keep it, and you remain free to use your contribution for
anything else.

The project is published under AGPL-3.0-only and also under a commercial
licence (`LICENSING.md`). Both are possible only if the licensor can grant
every part of the code under either, so the licence below is broad enough to
include contributions in the commercial edition, and to relicense them, on
any terms.

## How to sign

**As an individual.** Open a pull request. A bot checks whether you have
signed this version. If you have not, it asks you to comment on the pull
request with exactly this sentence:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the version of this agreement and the
time, and the check turns green. You sign once; it covers all your future
contributions under this version. If the bot does not pick up your comment,
comment `recheck`.

**For an organisation.** If you contribute as part of your job, or your
employer may own what you write, your employer must also agree, as the
"Entity" under section 9. An authorised signatory sends a signed copy of this
agreement, the organisation's legal name and address, and the GitHub
usernames of the people it authorises to contribute. To arrange this, open an
issue titled "Corporate CLA" (with no confidential details) and the
maintainer will reply with a private channel for the signed copy.
Each of those people then also signs as an individual on their first pull
request, as above. The organisation updates its list by writing through the same
channel.
